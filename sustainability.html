What is sustainability?

Sustainability means meeting our needs today without harming the ability of future generations to meet theirs

It focuses on using resources responsibly so they are not depleted or damaged

Sustainability is often described using the “triple bottom line”: people, planet and profit

Many everyday actions, like reducing energy use or cutting food waste, are part of sustainability because they conserve resources and lower environmental impact.

It encourages reducing waste, reusing materials, and recycling where possible.

Sustainability supports renewable resources instead of finite ones.

It aims for long‑term solutions, not short‑term gains.

    Sustainability balances three key areas:

    Environmental – protecting nature, reducing pollution, conserving energy and biodiversity.

    Social – supporting health, equality, education, and quality of life for people

    Economic – creating long‑term economic growth without causing environmental or social harm
