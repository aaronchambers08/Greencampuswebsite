Events & Activities

Green Week Challenge - May 22 2026 | Students are invited to join Green Week, a campus-wide challenge packed with daily eco-friendly activities. From zero‑waste lunch days to bike‑to‑school competitions, participants earn points for making sustainable choices and learning simple habits that reduce their environmental impact.

Green Week Challenge - July 14 2026 | Hands-on workshops run throughout the term, teaching practical skills like reusable tote making, basic bike repair, and upcycling old clothes. These short sessions are led by student volunteers and focus on easy, low-cost ways to live more sustainably every day.

Plant Trees near the river - july 20 2026 | Hands-on experience where over 50 baby trees will be planted near the river to help combat global warming. There will also be oppertunities to do some litter picking to clean up the area around the river.

    Community Clean & Grow Day - June 15 2026 | This monthly event brings students together to clean local green spaces and plant pollinator‑friendly flowers. After the clean-up, there’s a relaxed outdoor meetup with eco quizzes, plant swaps, and tips on how students can stay environmentally friendly both at school and at home.

    Planting flowers in the park - August 2 2026 | This will be a fun opportunity to plant flowers in the communal garden located in the park. Make sure that you bring some gloves!

    Greencampus Half-marathon - August 15 2026 | This 13.1 Mile (21 km) will be an event all about raising money for charity and reasearch about combatting global warming. People can make donations either at the event itself or in advance.
